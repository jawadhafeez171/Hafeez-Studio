# Studio homepage (/)

Scope: the studio marketing homepage, mode Persuade. Visitor: a business owner anywhere in the world, often on a mid-range Android phone, often in daylight. Action: book a discovery call or open WhatsApp. Proof: four live client sites with real phone screenshots. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no invented metrics; tutoring stays separate and keeps its own theme).

User decisions (2026-11-11): rejected the coral and plum look, the Garden City option and the bolder hand. Asked for "minimalistic" and for "beautiful typography". Took the category standard (canon), executed with care and led by type.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quiet, typographic studio page where well-set words, real client screens and honest prices do the persuading. It refuses coloured hero fields, card grids, pills, badges and eyebrows.

OWN-WORLD: Light neutral ground (#F8F8F6), near-black ink, one ink-blue accent (#2E3BC9) used only for links and the primary action. Source Serif 4 at display optical size for headlines (italic for the second clause), Hanken Grotesk for UI and body. Hairline rules instead of boxes, tabular numerals for prices, times and days, and generous whitespace on an 8px rhythm.

STORY: The visitor understands that one small studio builds the site and the follow-up, sees four live sites, reads a fixed price, and books a call or opens WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A minimal top bar (serif wordmark, four quiet links, a text "Book a call"). A left-aligned serif headline across about 70% of the width, one sentence of subcopy, a solid ink "Book a discovery call" button with a WhatsApp text link beside it, and one line of plain facts. The top of the client-screen row shows below the fold line.

FORM: category standard (canon), user-chosen, then steered to minimal and typographic. seed: 23817861791535b4. Signature: the hero headline settles in line by line on load, and the client phones lift on hover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
